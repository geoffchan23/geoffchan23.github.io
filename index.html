About

Hey, I'm Geoff. I've been in product and engineering for 15 years. I've run an agency, led engineering teams, and shipped products for some of the biggest brands in the world. Now, I'm back to building; fully focused on operationalizing agents that run real businesses.

At Jain, I lead product and engineering. We build AI that helps building service contractors bid and price their work. Most of my time goes to sitting with estimators and operators, learning how they price, and turning that into tools their whole team can use.

Before Jain, I was Head of Product & AI at Raven, building agents for manufacturers. I also founded Stack Five, a product agency I bootstrapped to ~$1M in revenue, and scaled and led a 16-person engineering team at Code4rena.

If you price, bid or run janitorial work, or just want to trade notes, find me on LinkedIn.
